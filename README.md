🚨 CivicEye – Geo-Tagged Civic Issue Reporting Platform

CivicEye is a MERN-based platform that allows citizens to report civic issues (like potholes, garbage, water leakage, etc.) with geo-tagging, images, and real-time tracking.
It helps authorities manage and resolve issues efficiently with role-based access control.

CiviEye – Community Driven Civic Issue Reporting Platform

See the issues. Share the solutions.
A modern civic engagement platform that empowers citizens to report local issues, track their status, and help build smarter communities.

📖 Overview

CiviEye is a full-stack web application that bridges the gap between citizens and local authorities by providing an easy-to-use platform for reporting civic issues such as:

🛣️ Potholes
💧 Water leakage
🗑️ Garbage collection
💡 Street light failures
🌳 Public property damage
🚦 Traffic-related issues
and many more...

Users can upload images, provide locations, interact with other reports, receive notifications, and monitor the progress of their submitted complaints.
